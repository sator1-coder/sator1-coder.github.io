URL
Exploring the role of AI in Early Childhood Education to improve Children's social skills
Site Author
 Jingyi Chen


Abstract
 The website explores the impact of children's social skills through personalized learning experiences supported by AI technology. It explains how different types of AI tools can help educate children, strengthening both their cognitive development and social abilities. On the website, research found that personalized learning not only improves children's motivation and self-confidence but also supports important social skills such as communication, teamwork, and emotional regulation. The website also highlights the challenges that arise when using AI with young children, including the issues of privacy, equity, and unnecessary resources. To establish that AI is used effectively and responsibly in early education, it is necessary to create a transparent, safe, and educationally appropriate learning ecosystem that supports children's overall growth.

 Game-based learning in Early Childhood education
Site Author
 Manar S Alotaibi
Abstract
 This site discusses game-based learning and how it has impacted children for many years. It has been a valuable tool for teaching different subjects and skills that support cognitive, social, and emotional development, as games help increase engagement, motivation, and enjoyment. The website provides more information about the effectiveness of game-based learning, recognizing that research recommends early childhood educators use it to enhance children's learning, while also acknowledging remaining gaps in the literature. Overall, this study supports children's development and learning outcomes by highlighting how game-based learning can be integrated into classroom experiences.
