URL
 AI Literacy in Early Childhood Education: The Challenges and Opportunities
Site Author
 Jiahong Su


Abstract
 This website explores how AI literacy is influencing the educational system, especially in Early Childhood Education, where AI curriculum development is still in progress. Research shows that educators and policymakers are discussing how to introduce AI literacy to young learners in ways that are meaningful and age-appropriate. Although creating a new AI-centered educational approach may face challenges, such as concerns on impacting children's learning but it also offers many opportunities. When we use AI tools wisely, they can support the children's growth, encourage curiosity, and enhance the younger learners' experiences. Overall, the site highlights both the benefits of AI and the ongoing efforts to integrate AI literacy into early childhood education.



URL
 Early Curriculum for AI enhancing the Quality of Education
Site Author
 Tr. Kalai Lakshmi
Abstract
 This site explores how the new generation should use AI because of the impact they had on education. AI has been widely used in secondary schools and universities, but there was no curriculum designed for Early Childhood Education. Now, AI has introduced an Early Childhood curriculum focused on AI Knowledge, AI Skills, AI Attitudes, and the inclusion of AI tools. The study shows that this curriculum helps young children understand AI tools and provides an effective teaching method for educators. It has also improved students' overall learning experiences.
