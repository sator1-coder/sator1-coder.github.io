Support Teachers With Administrative Tasks


URL
 Lighten teacher workloads and reduce burnout with AI designed for education
Site Author
 Sarah Grivakis


Abstract
 The website discusses how teachers can reduce stress through AI tools. In this new generation of technology, teachers can manage their workload better and still have meaningful time with their students. Recently, district leaders have been discussing how to create more learning opportunities, improve student outcomes, and support educators in managing their schedules. Research has found that AI has been a powerful tool for students' learning, creating perceptive instruction, and repetitive tasks. Research also shows concerns about the lack of data privacy for students. However, a new strategy of AI called Learning Intelligence Technology (LIT) has been helpful because it aligns with classroom standards and provides strong support for teachers.



URL
 AI for Educators: How district leaders can empower teachers with AI solutions
Site Author
 Leah Allen-Manning
Abstract
 This site discusses how AI has been impacting education within its system. It explains that using AI helps teachers save time on lesson planning, build stronger student engagement, reduce administrative workload, and improve attendance. Research shows that while AI cannot replace the expertise of teachers, it can help reduce their workload by creating lesson plans, activities, worksheets, and personalized data for students' academics, behaviors, and attendance. The site also covers additional topics, such as improving communication between families and educators, and emphasizing that the future of AI in education is already here. With these benefits, AI can be a helpful tool for district leaders and teachers.
